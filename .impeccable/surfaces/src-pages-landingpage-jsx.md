# Landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: padel players across Denmark who are not signed in. Action: "Opret gratis profil" (or "Gå til appen" when signed in). Proof: live public counts, upcoming public Americano events, Første 200 status. Constraint: only real data; no invented names or counts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page feels like the club house at night: the hero photo of the court fills the screen and the evening's offer is spoken in big condensed letters. It refuses the category default of a centred headline over a phone mockup and a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night-court ink ground (#1B1A17) with warm paper text (#F3EEE4), clay (#C8553D) for the action and accent words, ball-yellow (#E9B44C) for live signals. Anton uppercase headlines, Instrument Sans body, IBM Plex Mono only for data (times, counts, levels). 16-24 px radii, hairline dividers in paper at 12-15 % opacity.

STORY: The visitor sees a real court, reads "Find din makker. Spil i aften.", sees real counts, scrolls through three steps shown as app screens, sees what's on (events), and signs up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hero photo with slow zoom and parallax; logo top left, "Log ind" pill top right; bottom-left stack of a real live count line, the three-line Anton headline (74 px on phone, rising line by line), one sentence, and a full-width clay "Opret gratis profil" button; a ticker of real counts along the bottom edge.

FORM: Pinned by the owner (Claude Design prototype v4); not rolled. Signature interaction: sticky "Sådan virker det" with three app screens that cross-fade as you scroll, then a photo that opens from the centre via clip-path. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
